# 🌈 AI VisionTale

> **Every Picture Opens a New Fairytale! ✨**

AI VisionTale is an AI-powered storytelling web application that transforms images into magical children's stories. By combining **Computer Vision**, **Google Gemini AI**, and **Text-to-Speech**, the application creates personalized stories in multiple languages with an interactive and child-friendly interface.

# 🌟 About

AI VisionTale encourages children's imagination by turning their own pictures into creative stories. Users can upload or capture images, choose a story language and theme, and receive an AI-generated story with narration.

---

# 🎯 Aim

To develop an intelligent storytelling application that generates personalized children's stories from images using Artificial Intelligence while supporting multiple languages, themes, and audio narration.

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

Traditional storytelling applications provide fixed stories that cannot adapt to a child's imagination or uploaded images. Children deserve personalized stories that make them feel like part of the adventure.

---

# 💡 Solution

AI VisionTale analyzes uploaded images using Google Gemini AI and generates a unique story based on the selected language and theme. It also narrates the story using Text-to-Speech, making storytelling more engaging and interactive.

# ⚙ Workflow

1. Open AI VisionTale
2. Register/Login
3. Upload or Capture Images
4. Select Story Language
5. Select Story Theme
6. AI analyzes the images
7. Story is generated
8. Listen to narration
9. View Story History
